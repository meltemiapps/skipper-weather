Privacy Policy — Skipper Weather Study Guide (unofficial)

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Skipper Weather is an independent, unofficial study aid published by a private individual, Evangelos Neroladakis ("the author").

Not affiliated with any organisation

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or associated with the Royal Yachting Association (RYA) or any other sailing or boating organisation, training centre, examining body, national authority or meteorological service. The author is not a certified instructor. Organisation names appear only to make this statement and do not imply any relationship. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, language and unit settings are stored only on your device (in the app's local settings storage). They are not transmitted to the author or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Not for navigation

Content is for learning only and may contain errors. Never use the app for navigation, passage planning or safety decisions. Always use official forecasts and seek qualified instruction.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published at this address and in the app's source repository.
