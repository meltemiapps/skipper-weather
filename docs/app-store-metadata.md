**Description:**
Skipper Weather Study Guide is a free, open-source study guide for anyone learning marine weather at skipper level.

LEARN
• 8 modules and 16 lessons: pressure and wind, depressions and fronts, clouds and weather signs, fog and visibility, sea and land breezes, Beaufort and sea state, forecasts and terminology, and weather for passage planning
• Clear native diagrams and key-point summaries

PRACTISE
• 40 quiz questions with explanations
• Module quizzes and a mixed quiz, with best scores saved on your device

TOOLS
• Beaufort scale with speeds and probable wave heights
• Barometric tendency calculator
• Unit converter for wind, pressure, temperature, distance and height

YOUR WAY
• 16 languages, including Greek and Arabic (right to left)
• °C/°F, hPa/mb/inHg, knots/Beaufort/km/h/m/s/mph, nm/km/mi, m/ft
• Designed for iPhone and iPad with Liquid Glass

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Nothing leaves your device.

UNOFFICIAL
This app is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or associated with the Royal Yachting Association (RYA) or any other sailing organisation, training centre, examining body or meteorological service.

NOT FOR NAVIGATION
For learning only. Always use official forecasts and seek qualified instruction.
